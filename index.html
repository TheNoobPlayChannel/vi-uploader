Vi Uploader

Vi Uploader is a personal desktop tool. It is part of “Vi”, a private voice assistant that runs on the owner's own Windows PC. It lets the owner upload short videos that he created himself to his own TikTok accounts from the computer, instead of copying files to a phone.

What it does

  • The owner signs in to each of his own TikTok accounts with TikTok Login Kit (Desktop, PKCE). Access tokens are stored only on his PC.
  • When a video is ready, the app shows the video, the caption, the account and the privacy / interaction settings. The owner reviews them and presses “Publish” himself. Nothing is posted without that click.
  • It does not read, collect or analyse other users' data, and it does not post on behalf of anyone else.

It is not a public service: there are no customer accounts and no servers — everything runs locally.
