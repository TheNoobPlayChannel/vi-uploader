Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Vi Uploader (“the app”) is a personal desktop tool used by its owner on his own computer to upload his own videos to his own TikTok accounts.

Data the app uses

  • TikTok account access. After the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token, refresh token and the account's open ID, plus basic creator info (display name, avatar, allowed privacy options) needed to show the publishing screen.
  • Videos and captions that the owner chooses to upload.

Where it is stored

Only on the owner's PC (in the app's local data folder). The app has no server and does not send this data anywhere except to TikTok's official API to perform the upload the owner requested.

What the app does not do

  • No selling, sharing or transfer of data to third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No access to other users' content, messages or followers.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens and files.
